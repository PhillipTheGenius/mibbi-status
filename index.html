        What does this page check?

        The independent monitor checks the public website, sign-in page, Help center, Security page and whether signed-out visitors are blocked from listing private projects. Opening the sign-in page does not test signing in.

        Checks are scheduled about every 10 minutes and may be delayed by GitHub. Results older than 25 minutes show as out of date. Refreshing retrieves recorded results; it does not start a new monitoring run.

        These checks do not log in, buy anything, run a paid AI request or test every customer website. Payments, authenticated database actions, email delivery and AI generation need separate investigation.
